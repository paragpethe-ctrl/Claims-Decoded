## My format

- **Series name:** Fire Claims Decoded. Every reel carries it as a header,
  with the topic as a subtitle (e.g. "Fireworks and Cracker Shop Fires >>>").
- **Length:** 18 to 35 seconds.
- **Structure:** one occupancy or one cause per reel. Fire visual or hook
  line -> how the fire starts (the cause) -> 3 or 4 prevention steps, each
  with a short on-screen label -> CTA (like / follow / comment, or save).
- **On screen:** me talking to camera, or me in a circle cutout over b-roll
  of the hazard. Short label cards (2 to 5 words) for each cause and fix.
- **Engineer's angle:** explain why a fire spreads, not just that it does
  ("Fuel + Oxidiser = Fast Fire", blocked aisles stop firefighters).
- **Best line so far:** "Most fire claims start with something smaller than
  your thumb." Specific, visual, from the insurance side. More hooks like it.
- **Check AI-generated on-screen text before posting.** Reel 3 had garbled
  words in its graphics ("BUMPLAIFT", "Ansures a feer asice Requirement
  retirents?"). Every label must be real, correctly spelled text.

step to prevention.

from ever needing to make a fire insurance claim.
