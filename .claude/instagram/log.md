   godown mein aag lagi..."). It is the top reel, but n=1: a hint, not a rule.
   Never invent an incident to fit this shape.
2. **One reel per day, no reposts.** The 5 worst reels were all posted on 14 Nov
   within 6 hours, two of them duplicates; all stayed under 170 views. Cadence
   likely mattered more than wording.
3. **The claim angle gets shared.** "Your fire insurance claim can be reduced or
   rejected..." has 11 shares, the next best has 5. Use the insurance-claim
   consequence as the stakes line.
4. **"Save this" is not converting yet.** No reel has more than 2 saves. Give a
   concrete reason to save (a checklist, numbers to check) or use a different ask.
5. **Hook shape is a tendency at best.** Heavy crossover between groups; the best
   generic warning (1,942) beats almost everything. Re-run this audit after
   15 to 20 more reels.
