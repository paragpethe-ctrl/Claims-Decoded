# Collab outreach: partner shortlist and pitch messages

Made with /ig-dm and checked with /ig-human. Send every message by hand from @fireclaimsdecoded. Never use a tool that sends DMs automatically.

## How to pick the actual accounts

- **Size:** 5k to 50k followers. They reply to DMs, and their audience is engaged.
- **Indian audience:** most comments come from India.
- **Engaged comments:** real questions in the comments, not just emojis.
- **Neighbouring niche only:** never another fire-insurance or claims creator. That's how you keep your niche.
- **Comment first:** leave useful comments on 3 to 5 of their posts over about 2 weeks before you DM. A warm pitch gets far more replies than a cold one.
- **Fill in {the specific post}** with a real post of theirs that you actually watched. Never invent one.
- **Share 1 Reel idea only.** Don't share your series plan, topic list or content bank.

- **What they get:** They get a fresh claim angle that adds to their awareness content, plus a post on both grids that reaches both audiences

## Follow-ups (same 2 for every partner)

**Day +4, only if there's no reply (225 characters):**

```
Hi {name}, one new thing since last time. I've written the full 30-second script for the Reel, and your part is just 2 short lines that you could record on your phone in about 10 minutes, wherever you are. Want me to send it?
```

**Day +10, the last message (262 characters):**

```
Hi {name}, I'll stop here. I know December is a packed month for anyone posting, with Christmas and New Year content on top of the usual work. If a collab with Fire Claims Decoded ever fits your plans, the offer stands. Either way, I'll keep watching your Reels.
```

Then stop. A third message won't get a reply and can hurt the relationship.

## If they say yes

1. Send the 30-second script with their 2 lines marked.
2. Use Instagram's **Invite collaborator** option when you post, so the Reel appears on both grids.
3. You keep the claim explanation in your own words and on your own face.
4. Agree on who posts and when, and reply to comments together for the first hour.
