# Vapour Cloud Explosion: Gemini video prompts

Three clips, about 10 seconds each, joined in order in your editor. English.

Before each prompt: upload the same clear, front-facing photo of yourself, so
the presenter looks the same in all three clips. Wear the same outfit in that
photo every time.

## Script

```
Smell gas? Stop. Don't touch your light switch.
LPG is up to 2 times as heavy as air.
It creeps along the floor into a cloud you can't see.
1 spark. The whole cloud goes up at once.
That's a vapour cloud explosion. A blast, not a fire.
Fit gas detectors with auto shut-off.
Check hoses and valves every 30 days.
Flameproof fittings near gas.
Close the valve, open the doors.
Walk out. Never the switch.
Save this before you smell gas.
```

## Part 1 (0:00 to 0:10): hook and the cloud

```
Vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, photorealistic, cinematic.

Use the uploaded photo as the presenter. Keep the face exactly as in the photo.
The presenter wears a white shirt with thin blue vertical stripes.

Shot 1 (0 to 3 seconds): a dim LPG cylinder storage room in an Indian
industrial unit, rows of red LPG cylinders along the wall. The presenter stands
by the door, one hand raised and stopped just short of a white wall light
switch, and looks straight into the camera. Speaks in English, loudly but
calmly, with conviction:
"Smell gas? Stop. Don't touch your light switch."

Shot 2 (3 to 10 seconds): camera at floor level. A faint, transparent shimmer,
like heat haze, flows out from a cylinder valve and spreads low across the
concrete floor, rolling slowly under shelves and towards the door. No smoke, no
colour: the gas is nearly invisible. The presenter's voice continues
off-screen:
"LPG is up to two times as heavy as air. It creeps along the floor into a cloud
you can't see."

Audio: a soft, steady hiss of escaping gas under the presenter's voice. No music.

No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Part 2 (0:10 to 0:20): the spark and the blast

```
Vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, photorealistic, cinematic.

Use the uploaded photo as the presenter. Keep the face exactly as in the photo.
The presenter wears a white shirt with thin blue vertical stripes, the same as
in the previous clip.

Shot 1 (0 to 2 seconds): extreme close-up of an electric motor starter box on
the wall of an empty industrial shed. A small blue-white spark jumps inside it.

Shot 2 (2 to 6 seconds): wide shot of the empty shed, packed with pipes and
machinery. The invisible cloud near the floor ignites everywhere at once: a
fast, rolling orange fireball fills the space, the pressure wave shakes the
pipework and blows out the windows. No people anywhere in this shot.

Shot 3 (6 to 10 seconds): the presenter stands outside in daylight at a safe
distance, with the scorched, smoking shed and broken windows behind, facing the
camera. Throughout the clip the presenter speaks in English, loudly but calmly
(off-screen in shots 1 and 2, on camera in shot 3):
"One spark. The whole cloud goes up at once. That's a vapour cloud explosion.
A blast, not a fire."

Audio: a sharp electrical crack on the spark, then a deep boom and rumble,
then the voice, clear and in sync with the lips in shot 3.

No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```
