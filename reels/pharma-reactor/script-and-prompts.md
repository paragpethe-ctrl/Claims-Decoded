# Pharma Reactor Explosions (revised for reach)

**Hook:** "Can 1 reactor cost you everything? Bharuch lost 5 workers." Hook score 100 (old version: 88).
**Checks:** /ig-reel beat sheet clean, last line loops back to the hook; /ig-human PASS 77; caption /ig-caption READY, /ig-human PASS 91.
**Length:** 4 clips, about 32 seconds spoken plus a 2s end card.

## Script

1. Can 1 reactor cost you everything? / Bharuch lost 5 workers. / In October 2026, a 15-kilolitre reactor failed there during hydrogenation. / Telangana lost 2.
2. In Bharuch and Telangana, officials haven't confirmed the causes yet. / Here's the risk. / Overheat a reaction, and pressure can build faster than vents release.
3. In India, fire policies usually name explosion as a covered peril. / Lost production isn't. / That needs separate business interruption cover, so check your schedule.
4. Fix 1: call insurers. / Keep batch records. / Send this to your plant head before 1 reactor costs everything.

## What changed from the old version, and why

| old | new | why it helps reach |
|---|---|---|
| Opens on "Five workers killed" | Opens on a question to the viewer, then the fact | Viewers stop for a question about their own risk; leading with deaths reads as news and gets scrolled |
| Safety only | Adds the insurance angle (explosion usually covered, lost production needs business interruption cover) | That's the series promise, and the part only an insurance engineer can say |
| Implied the cause right after the incidents | Says the causes aren't confirmed, then explains the general risk | Accurate, and protects the account's credibility |
| No ask | "Send this to your plant head" | Sends are the strongest reach signal; plant heads and safety officers forward this kind of post |
| No loop | Last line repeats "1 reactor ... everything" | Replays feel seamless |
| Gold frame, "5 + 2 KILLED", realistic blast with flying debris | Titles only, softer impact words, gauge and 3D cutaway instead of a blast | Matches your current format; a realistic recreation of a named, recent, fatal blast is what made Gemini refuse the Surat reel |
| 3 clips | 4 clips (adds the insurance clip) | Room for the claim angle without cramming |

## Facts (checked in 2+ sources)

- Bharuch (SynZeal Pharmachem, Saykha GIDC, Vagra taluka), 16 Oct 2026, about 10 pm: a 15 kL reactor failed during hydrogenation; 5 workers killed; injured reported as 5 or 6 (left out); methylene dichloride released; cause not established, industrial safety officials investigating.
- Telangana (Hazelo Lab, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri), 21 Aug 2026: 2 dead; injured reported as 8 or 9 (left out); management said likely overheating; police have not established the cause.
- Insurance points: explosion is a named peril in Indian standard fire policies; loss of profits and consequential loss are excluded and need business interruption cover. The exact treatment of damage to the reactor or pressure vessel itself wasn't verified, so the script says "may be claimable" and "check your schedule".
- General information, not financial or legal advice. Check your own policy.

Sources: [DD News](https://newsonair.gov.in/gujarat-five-workers-killed-five-injured-in-reactor-explosion-at-chemical-factory/), [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/10/17/five-workers-killed-in-gujarat-chemical-blast-what-caused-the-explosion.amp.html), [Deccan Chronicle (Bharuch)](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/nation/gujarat-five-killed-six-injured-in-reactor-blast-at-synzeel-pharmachem-company-1996576), [DD News (Telangana)](https://newsonair.gov.in/two-dead-nine-injured-in-reactor-blast-at-hazelo-labs-in-telangana/), [Deccan Chronicle (Telangana)](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/reactor-blast-in-hazelo-pharma-in-bhongir-two-workers-dead-1981234), [Tata AIG: SFSP perils](https://www.tataaig.com/sme-insurance/fire-burglary-insurance/standard-fire-and-special-perils-policy)

Reactor explosion insurance: would your policy pay for lost production? Bharuch lost 5 workers in October.

In October 2026, a 15-kilolitre reactor failed during hydrogenation at a pharma unit in Bharuch's Saykha GIDC area, and a toxic solvent gas leaked after it. Telangana lost 2 more in August. Causes aren't confirmed yet.

Overheat a reaction, and pressure can build faster than your vents can release it.

Here's the insurance side. In India, fire policies usually name explosion as a covered peril, so the damage itself may be claimable. Lost production isn't. That needs separate business interruption cover, and you'll find out too late if your schedule doesn't list it.

⚠️ Here's what works. Tell your insurer that same day. Keep batch records, temperature logs and photos. Don't clear the site.

Share this with your plant head or safety officer.
