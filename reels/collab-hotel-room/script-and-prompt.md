# Collab Reel: First 30 seconds in any hotel room

A 20-second collab Reel with a travel vlogger. He films 2 short real clips on his next trip. You make 1 animated clip in Gemini with THE EXPERT. You join them in CapCut and post it as an Instagram Collab, so it appears on both profiles.

- **Hook:** "Ruko! Don't unpack before you find 2 exits." Hook score 100.
- **Language:** Hinglish, matching his travel audience.

## Filming brief for the vlogger

Send him this as it is:

> Hi {name}, here's all I need, about 15 minutes on your next hotel check-in:
>
> **Clip 1 (3 seconds):** Film from the corridor as you open your room door and walk in. You're about to throw your bag on the bed, then you stop, turn to the camera and say: "Ruko! Don't unpack before you find 2 exits."
>
> **Clip 2 (5 seconds):** Walk from your room door to the nearest EXIT sign. Touch each door on the way with your hand and count out loud: "1, 2, 3, 4... exit. Done. 30 seconds." End standing under the exit sign.
>
> **Clip 3 (2 seconds):** Smile at the camera: "Save karo, next trip ke liye."
>
> Shoot vertical (9:16) in daylight or normal corridor light. Your usual vlog style is perfect. Send me the raw clips, and I'll do the editing and send you the final video before we post.

Don't film other guests' faces, or room numbers that show the hotel's name.
