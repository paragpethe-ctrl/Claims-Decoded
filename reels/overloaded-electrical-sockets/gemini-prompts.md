# Episode 02: Overloaded Electrical Socket Fire, Gemini video prompts

Four clips of about 10 seconds each, about 40 seconds in total. Upload the
same photo of yourself before every prompt and join the clips in order.

Every prompt carries the same FRAME block, word for word, so the series
frame looks identical in all four clips. Do not edit it in one prompt only.

## Script at a glance

| part | narration | spoken |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop. Heater and iron on one 10-amp board? That's 13 amps. Thirty percent over its limit. | ~6.5s |
| 2 | More current, more heat. The plug softens. Insulation melts. The curtain behind can catch fire. Warning signs: a hot plug, a burning smell. | ~9.2s |
| 3 | Check the amp rating printed on your board. Never run two heavy appliances from one socket. Need more points? Call a qualified electrician. | ~9.2s |
| 4 | A fire claim can take months. Prevention takes one minute. So tell me: how many appliances share one socket in your home? | ~8.8s |

The maths in the hook: a 2,000 W heater plus a 1,000 W iron is 3,000 W.
At 230 V that is 13 A, through a board rated for 10 A: 30% over.
