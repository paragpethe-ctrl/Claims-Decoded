# Stop / Ruko / Avoid: 15 clips from real rejected and disputed fire claims

Every clip is built on a real Indian Supreme Court, NCDRC or consumer commission case from 2024–26. Real cases get more reach than general tips, because each one has a number, a twist and a lesson the viewer can act on.

- Every hook was checked with `/ig-reel` hookscore: all 15 score **92.6 to 100**.
- Narration is Hinglish, 22 to 27 words, so it fits in 10 seconds.
- Clip structure: 0–3 s THE EXPERT stops the mistake · 3–6 s case infographic · 6–8 s night consequence · 8–10 s the lesson.
- **No company, insurer or person is named on screen or in the narration.** That keeps Gemini from refusing a real-event prompt. Credit the case in the caption (the case line and sources are under each prompt).

Attach your reference photo with every prompt. Add to every caption: "General information, not financial or legal advice. Check your own policy."
