# Stop / Ruko: 10 one-clip Reels (10 seconds each)

Format taken from the reference video (an illustrated HSE Reel, 10 seconds):
1. **0–3 s, the mistake:** a worker doing something unsafe.
2. **3–6 s, the stop:** an Indian safety officer in a hi-vis vest turns to the camera, raises his palm and says "Ruko/Stop", then points.
3. **6–10 s, the fix:** the right way to do it, and a smile or nod at the end.

The art is a clean 2D/3D illustrated cartoon. A cartoon also makes a Gemini refusal much less likely than photoreal footage.

Each prompt below is complete and can be pasted straight into Gemini. Narration is Hinglish, written in Roman script so the captions stay readable, and runs 18–22 words so it fits about 8.5 seconds.

---
