# The Refuge Area That Became a Store Room

**Hook:** "Never block your one refuge area. Nalasopara lost two lives." Hook score 100 (length, specificity, stakes, front-loading and viewer address all 100).
**Timing (beats.py):** 24.7s spoken across 9 beats: hook beat 1.8s, no beat over 4s, a concrete number or name in every run of beats, last line loops back to "refuge area". Plus a 2s end card.
**Humanizer:** fingerprint 100, slop 100 (no em dashes, invisible characters or AI filler).

## Script

1. Never block your one refuge area. Nalasopara lost two lives. The fire reached the refuge floor door itself. Mumbai, November: a refuge area burned, beside scrap. (9.1s)
2. Above 24 metres, ladders can't reach everyone. In Goregaon, residents waited safely on the 15th-floor refuge. Fill yours with junk, and it becomes fuel. (8.7s)
3. Check 3 things every month: empty, unlocked, signposted. Do you know which floor your refuge area is on? (6.5s)

## Facts to check before posting

- Mumbai, 8 Nov 2026 (PTI, via Siasat): in one of three high-rise fires, the fire was confined to a dry refuge area and an old scrap DG set on the ground floor of a 22-storey building. The report doesn't make clear which building. [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/three-mumbai-high-rises-hit-by-fires-no-injuries-reported-3571459/)
- Nalasopara (Imperial Heights), Oct 2026: 2 women dead; the door to the 12th-floor refuge floor was engulfed in flames. Nothing reported says storage caused this fire; the script doesn't claim it did. [National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/amp/story/national/mumbai-two-women-killed-dozens-evacuated-as-fire-sweeps-nalasopara-high-rise)
- Goregaon, 2026: residents moved to a 15th-floor refuge area as a precaution. [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/fire-breaks-out-in-mumbai-high-rise-no-injuries-five-rescued/2900151/)
- Refuge areas start at the 24 m mark in Indian rules; the spacing above that differs by code and city (every 7th floor vs every 15 m), so the script uses only "above 24 metres". The Mumbai Fire Brigade document says a refuge area shall not be used for any other purpose. [Infralens: NBC 2016 Part 4](https://infralens.in/knowledge/nbc-2016-part-4-fire-safety), [Mumbai Fire Brigade (PDF)](https://environmentclearance.nic.in/DownloadPfdFile.aspx?FileName=GGJqliViVE6VcYAsGWsGuSrrSZbEKM00rE8Z9nk3wiFY+gQJIR6lJ6ZZelE9N7UiHmdeXYLsNdlV0mMTsjLh0A%3D%3D&FilePath=93ZZBm8LWEXfg+HAlQix2fE2t8z%2FpgnoBhDlYdZCxzWYSktLmWVaXKfetCrs6kw3DoGTia+2sdXMwhPpplwBWg9RbosgeqKp3InPnjsjC8Q%3D)
