## Script

1. Can 1 spark cost your godown everything? / Surat lost lakhs. / On 14 November, a textile waste warehouse there went up fast. / Over 10 tenders.
2. In Surat, fabric stacked tight burned faster than crews could reach. / Cause still unknown. / Wiring and sparks near your stock are always the classic risk.
3. Fix 1: rewire. / Declare your storage honestly, or your claim becomes a dispute. / Could 1 spark cost your godown everything?

## Facts to check before posting

- Kharwasa Canal Road area, Surat, Saturday 14 Nov 2026: textile (cloth) waste warehouse fire; Sub-Fire Officer said 10 to 12 fire tenders and 50 to 60 personnel; goods worth lakhs damaged; large stacks of textile material let the flames spread quickly; cause unknown.
- One outlet (India TV) reported a death and a third-floor origin; no other source confirmed either, so the script mentions neither.
- "Declare your storage honestly": how undeclared or changed storage affects a claim depends on the policy wording.
