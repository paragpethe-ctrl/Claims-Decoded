# Restaurant Kitchen Fires: Gemini video prompts

Three clips, about 10 seconds each, joined in order in your editor.

Before each prompt: upload the same clear, front-facing photo of yourself, so
the presenter looks the same in all three clips. Wear the same outfit in that
photo every time.

---

## Part 1 (0:00 to 0:10): hook

```
Vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, photorealistic, cinematic.

Use the uploaded photo as the presenter. Keep his face exactly as in the photo.
He wears a white shirt with thin blue vertical stripes.

Shot 1 (0 to 2 seconds): extreme close-up of a large iron kadhai on a
commercial gas burner in a busy Indian restaurant kitchen. Oil smokes, and a
flame flares up from the kadhai. The camera tilts up quickly to a stainless
steel exhaust hood above it, its metal filters coated in thick, dark, sticky
grease.

Shot 2 (2 to 10 seconds): the camera pulls back to reveal the presenter
standing in the kitchen in front of the hood, looking straight into the
camera. Cooks work behind him and steam rises. He speaks in Hindi, loudly but
calmly, with conviction, and points up at the hood on the second line:
"किचन की चिमनी को इग्नोर करना बंद कीजिए। रेस्टोरेंट की आग वहीं छुपी होती है। रोज़ का तड़का, रोज़ का तेल। सब डक्ट में जमता है।"

Audio: his voice, clear and in sync with his lips, over sizzling oil and
kitchen clatter.

No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Part 2 (0:10 to 0:20): what happens

```
Vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, photorealistic, cinematic.

Use the uploaded photo as the presenter. Keep his face exactly as in the photo.
He wears a white shirt with thin blue vertical stripes, the same as in the
previous clip.

Shot 1 (0 to 4 seconds): inside a greasy metal kitchen exhaust duct. A single
spark drifts up from below and lands on the grease, which catches fire. The
flame races along the duct, upward and away from the camera, toward the roof.

Shot 2 (4 to 10 seconds): the presenter stands at a stainless steel kitchen
sink, facing the camera, holding up a clean, shiny metal hood filter in one
hand and a greasy dark one in the other. He speaks in Hindi, loudly but
calmly, with conviction:
"एक चिंगारी, और वो तेल जल उठता है। ये आग सीधा छत तक जाती है। फ़िल्टर हर 7 दिन साफ़ कीजिए। डक्ट की सफ़ाई हर 3 महीने।"

Audio: a low whoosh as the duct ignites, then his voice, clear and in sync
with his lips.

No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Part 3 (0:20 to 0:30): prevention and ask

```
Vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, photorealistic, cinematic.

Use the uploaded photo as the presenter. Keep his face exactly as in the photo.
He wears a white shirt with thin blue vertical stripes, the same as in the
previous clips.

Shot 1 (0 to 3 seconds): close-up of hands running along an orange LPG rubber
hose to the regulator on a gas cylinder in a restaurant kitchen, checking it.

Shot 2 (3 to 6 seconds): a kadhai of oil catches fire on the stove. A hand
calmly slides a heavy metal lid over it and the flame goes out. Nobody uses
water.

Shot 3 (6 to 10 seconds): the presenter stands in the clean kitchen next to a
red fire extinguisher mounted on the wall, faces the camera, smiles and
points up toward the exhaust hood on the last line. Throughout the clip he
speaks in Hindi, loudly but calmly:
"LPG पाइप हर महीने चेक कीजिए। कढ़ाई में आग? पानी नहीं, ढक्कन रखिए। K-type एक्सटिंग्विशर पास रखिए। सेव कीजिए, और कल पहले चिमनी देखिए।"

Audio: his voice, clear, over quiet kitchen sounds.

No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
```
