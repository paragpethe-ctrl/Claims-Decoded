## Facts and where they come from

Check these again on the day you post; death tolls and causes were still
being updated.

- **Siwan, Bihar (around 22 October 2026):** blasts at a house in Alampur
  village where firecrackers were stored ahead of Diwali. Police said a large
  quantity of firecrackers was stored inside. Reports said explosions went on
  for hours. The death toll was reported between 3 and 8 at different times
  (latest seen: 6), so the script gives no number.
  [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261023/60019c95ea65471c8d963b13ea79d967/c.html),
  [News24](https://news24online.com/india/bihar-horror-deadly-siwan-firecracker-blast-rips-apart-house-7-dead-as-explosions-continue-for-hours/946260),
  [Patna Press](https://patnapress.com/siwan-firecracker-blast-death-toll-rises-six-body-found/)
- **Near Namakkal, Tamil Nadu (around 1 October 2026):** fire at a firecracker
  godown near Pandamangalam. The owner and his wife died at the scene; two
  workers died later in hospital.
  [Afternoon News](https://afternoonnews.in/article/four-killed-in-fire-at-firecracker-godown-near-namakkal/)
