# Cracker Shop and Godown Fires: Gemini video prompts

Five clips, about 38 seconds in total. Upload the same photo of yourself
before every prompt. Join the clips in order. The FRAME block is identical in
every prompt; do not edit it in one prompt only.

## Script

| part | narration | spoken |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Never store Diwali crackers at home. Siwan, Bihar, in October shows why. | ~4.8s |
| 2. Siwan | Police say a large cracker stock was kept inside a house. It exploded. The blasts went on for hours. | ~7.6s |
| 3. Namakkal | Weeks earlier, near Namakkal, a cracker godown caught fire. The owner and his wife died there. | ~6.4s |
| 4. Why | Crackers carry their own oxygen. Once they catch, you can't smother them. One spark becomes a chain reaction. | ~7.2s |
| 5. Fix, question, loop | Licence. Stock limits. Sand and extinguishers ready. Is there a cracker godown in your lane? Is it licensed? Because crackers never belong at home. | ~9.6s |

## Why this should travel

- **Frame 1 is motion**, not a face: a fuse catching inside a home cupboard.
- **The hook is a command plus a real, recent place.** Scored 81.8 STRONG.
- **Every 2 to 3 seconds the shot changes.** No static frame longer than 4s.
- **Real incidents, stated as police reported them.** Credible and shareable.
- **A quiet moment of respect** (one diya) in part 3: people share what moves
  them, and it keeps the tone right for a story where people died.
- **The engineer's "why"** in part 4 is the part only you can say.
- **One ask: a question about the viewer's own lane.** Easy to answer in a
  comment, and people tag neighbours.
- **The loop:** the last line repeats "crackers", "never", "home" from the
  first, and the last shot matches the first, so the replay is seamless.
- **Timing:** post before Diwali (8 November) while the topic is searched.
